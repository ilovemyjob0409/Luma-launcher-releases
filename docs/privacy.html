Luma Launcher 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 27 日　｜　開發者：韋德　｜　聯絡信箱：wade1231kung@gmail.com

Luma Launcher（套件名稱 com.luma.launcher，以下稱「Luma」）是一款 Android 桌面啟動器。我們重視你的隱私：Luma 不會收集、上傳、出售或與任何第三方分享你的個人資料。Luma 沒有帳號系統、沒有廣告、也沒有使用任何分析或追蹤工具。

1. 我們收集哪些資料

我們不收集任何可識別你身分的資料。Luma 運作時需要讀取的資料（例如下方權限所列），全部只在你的手機上處理與儲存，不會傳送到我們或任何第三方的伺服器。

2. 權限用途說明

以下權限都只在你啟用相關功能時使用，資料只留在裝置上：

權限用途
無障礙服務（手勢服務）在其他 App 上方顯示 Luma 的懸浮狀態列、橫幅通知、通知中心與控制中心面板，並偵測下拉手勢。Luma 不會利用此服務讀取或記錄你在其他 App 中輸入的內容。
通知存取權讀取通知以顯示 iPhone 風格的橫幅通知、通知中心及 App 未讀數字。通知內容不會離開你的手機。
使用情況存取權判斷目前前景的 App，以便切換狀態列顏色、顯示最近使用的 App 等介面功能。
聯絡人、行事曆在桌面搜尋與小工具中顯示你的聯絡人與行程。
位置（大略／精確）、藍牙掃描、Wi-Fi 與網路狀態顯示狀態列與控制中心中的網路、Wi-Fi、藍牙等資訊，以及需要位置的介面功能。
螢幕錄影（媒體投影）、麥克風僅在你主動使用控制中心的「螢幕錄影」功能時使用；錄影檔只存在你的手機中，Luma 不會上傳。
生物辨識你自行設定的 App 鎖定／隱藏功能。驗證由 Android 系統處理，Luma 不會取得你的指紋或臉部資料。
網際網路開啟你選擇的搜尋引擎或網站、以及透過 Google Play 驗證購買狀態。Luma 本身不會把你的資料傳到任何伺服器。
修改系統設定、通知勿擾權限讓控制中心可以調整亮度、勿擾模式等你主動操作的設定。

3. 購買

Luma 提供免費試用，試用後可透過 Google Play 一次購買完整版。付款完全由 Google Play 處理，我們不會取得你的信用卡或付款資訊；Luma 只會從 Google Play 取得「是否已購買」的狀態。Google 如何處理付款資料，請參考 Google 隱私權政策。

4. 第三方服務

當你在 Luma 的搜尋列輸入文字並選擇搜尋引擎或 AI 服務時，Luma 會用瀏覽器開啟該服務的網頁。之後的資料處理由該服務依其隱私權政策負責，Luma 不會另外記錄你的搜尋內容。

5. 資料保存與刪除

Luma 的設定、桌面配置與備份都存在你的手機上。你可以隨時在 Android 設定中清除 Luma 的資料，或直接解除安裝，即可刪除所有資料。

6. 兒童隱私

Luma 不是為 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的個人資料。

7. 政策變更

如果本政策有更新，我們會修改本頁並更新上方的生效日期。

8. 聯絡我們

Luma Launcher Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026 | Developer: 韋德 (Wade) | Contact: wade1231kung@gmail.com

Luma Launcher (package name com.luma.launcher, "Luma") is a home screen launcher for Android. Luma does not collect, upload, sell, or share your personal data with anyone. Luma has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

1. Data we collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. Any data Luma needs to read in order to work (see the permissions below) is processed and stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers or to any third party.

2. How permissions are used

These permissions are only used when you turn on the related feature, and the data stays on your device:

PermissionPurpose
Accessibility service (gesture service)Shows Luma's floating status bar, banner notifications, Notification Center and Control Center panels on top of other apps, and detects pull-down gestures. Luma does not use this service to read or record what you type in other apps.
Notification accessReads notifications to show iPhone-style banners, the Notification Center, and app badges. Notification content never leaves your device.
Usage accessDetects the foreground app to adapt the status bar color and to show recently used apps.
Contacts, CalendarShows your contacts and events in home screen search and widgets.
Location (approximate/precise), Bluetooth scan, Wi-Fi and network stateShows network, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth information in the status bar and Control Center, and powers interface features that need location.
Screen recording (media projection), MicrophoneUsed only when you start the Screen Recording feature from Control Center. Recordings are saved on your device and are never uploaded by Luma.
BiometricsFor the app lock / hidden apps feature you set up yourself. Authentication is handled by Android; Luma never receives your fingerprint or face data.
InternetOpens the search engine or website you choose, and checks your purchase status with Google Play. Luma itself does not send your data to any server.
Modify system settings, Do Not Disturb accessLets Control Center change brightness, Do Not Disturb and similar settings when you tap them.

3. Purchases

Luma offers a free trial, after which you can unlock the full version with a one-time purchase on Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never receive your card or payment details. Luma only receives whether you have purchased the full version. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles payment data.

4. Third-party services

When you type in Luma's search bar and choose a search engine or AI service, Luma opens that service's website in your browser. From then on your data is handled by that service under its own privacy policy. Luma does not keep a record of your searches.

5. Data retention and deletion

Luma's settings, home screen layout and backups are stored on your device. You can delete all of it at any time by clearing Luma's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

6. Children's privacy

Luma is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
